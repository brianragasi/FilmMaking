Section 3
An Eight-Minute DDoS Awareness Screenplay
Cast of Characters
SARAH – A practical and determined student who waits for the
EcoCart sale with her classmates. She becomes one of the
many legitimate customers affected when the website suddenly
stops working.
SELLER / ECOCART OWNER – The person responsible for the
business and its online store. Concerned about the failed
sale, the seller later calls an incident meeting to
understand what happened and how the company will prevent
another attack.
STORE MANAGER – The manager responsible for sales
operations, employees, and customer service. Confident
before the sale, but immediately concerned when the website
becomes unavailable.
SERVER ADMIN 1 – A member of EcoCart's technical team who
monitors server performance, identifies the abnormal traffic
spike, and helps restore the service.
SERVER ADMIN 2 – A second technical specialist who filters
malicious traffic, coordinates with the security provider,
and explains the company's recovery actions.
CYBERSECURITY ANALYST – The specialist who confirms that the
event is a Distributed Denial-of-Service attack and explains
the botnet, the threat, and the recommended preventive
controls.
INCIDENT RESPONSE LEAD – The person who coordinates the
company's response, records the actions taken, and
recommends a clear incident response plan for future
emergencies.
ATTACKER – A hidden figure who controls a group of
compromised devices and orders them to flood the EcoCart
server with repeated requests.
2
CLASSMATES / STUDENTS – Ten to twelve students who discuss
the Big Blowout Sale, prepare their carts, and later react
when their purchases cannot be completed.
CONSTRUCTION WORKERS – Five to six workers resting during
their break. They hope to buy discounted safety equipment,
tools, and work supplies.
PICK ME RIDER – A ride-hailing rider who plans to buy
motorcycle accessories and rain gear before returning to
work.
NANAYS AND BARANGAY RESIDENTS – Six to eight neighbors who
begin by discussing a local barangay issue, but quickly
change the topic when the online sale starts.
MOTHER AND FAMILY MEMBERS – A mother caring for an infant,
supported by two relatives or friends. She hopes the sale
will help her save money on essential baby products.
STORE EMPLOYEES AND CUSTOMERS – Five to seven ensemble
members who prepare orders, assist shoppers, and show the
scale of EcoCart's expected sale activity.
BOTNET REPRESENTATIVES – Students who visually represent
infected phones, computers, and other devices repeatedly
sending fake requests. These roles may be played by actors
from earlier scenes.
ENSEMBLE / MULTI-ROLE ACTORS:
● Classmates: Ten to twelve students who discuss the Big
Blowout Sale, prepare their carts, and later react when
their purchases cannot be completed.
● Construction Workers (wearing safety vests): Five to
six workers resting during their break. They hope to
buy discounted safety equipment, tools, and work
supplies.
● Store Employees and Customers : Five to seven ensemble
members who prepare orders, assist shoppers, and show
the scale of EcoCart's expected sale activity.
● Botnet Representatives (holding red screens): Students
who visually represent infected phones, computers, and
other devices repeatedly sending fake requests. These
roles may be played by actors from earlier scenes.
● Barangay Neighbors: Six to eight neighbors who begin by
discussing a local barangay issue, but quickly change
the topic when the online sale starts.
3
● Delivery Rider: A ride-hailing rider who plans to buy
motorcycle accessories and rain gear before returning
to work.
● Mother with Infant and Family (using props): A mother
caring for an infant, supported by two relatives or
friends. She hopes the sale will help her save money on
essential baby products.
Setting:
A busy weekday at noon during EcoCart's largest online
promotion. The story moves between a classroom, an EcoCart
store, a construction site, a barangay waiting shed, a small
family home, Sarah's bedroom, an unknown attacker's room,
EcoCart's IT office, and the company conference room. The
locations may be recorded separately and connected through
quick cuts, split-screen shots, phone close-ups, and short
montage sequences.
At Rise:
People from different parts of the community are waiting for
the EcoCart Big Blowout Sale. Students have prepared their
shopping carts. Workers are looking for affordable
equipment. Parents are hoping to save money on household
needs. Inside EcoCart, employees expect a record number of
orders. None of them knows that an attacker is preparing to
flood the website with malicious traffic.
Production Note:
The screenplay is designed for approximately thirty to forty
students and a total video duration of about eight minutes.
Ensemble actors may appear in more than one short scene.
Dialogue should be delivered naturally, while transitions
and reaction shots should remain quick to protect the
running time.
Budget Filming Strategy:
● EcoCart HQ / Store: Filmed in an empty campus
classroom or org room. Stack 8–10 plain
cardboard boxes taped with printed "EcoCart"
paper logos to simulate a fulfillment hub.
● Construction Site: Shot outdoors against a
brick/concrete campus wall. Actors wear bright
safety vests over casual clothes.
● Delivery Rider: Shot in the campus parking lot
next to a parked scooter or motorcycle.
4
● Barangay Shed: Shot at any campus gazebo,
outdoor bench, or covered walkway.
● Family Home: Dorm room corner. The baby is a
swaddled towel/blanket bundle held close to
the actor’s chest (camera shot over the
shoulder).
● IT Command Center: Dimly lit campus computer
lab or desk with laptops displaying
code/network graphs.

CHAPTER 4 – INCIDENT RESPONSE AND RECOVERY
INT. ECOCART IT OFFICE – DAY
Warning messages appear across the technical team's
monitors. One graph rises sharply beyond its normal range.
SERVER ADMIN 1 and SERVER ADMIN 2 compare traffic logs while
the STORE MANAGER waits behind them.
SERVER ADMIN 1
Website traffic has increased far beyond
normal levels.
SERVER ADMIN 2
Oo. Kadaghanan sa requests gikan sa

STORE MANAGER

SERVER ADMIN 1
Sa karon, wala pa mi nakita nga naay
na-steal nga customer accounts. Murag
Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS)
attack ni.
STORE MANAGER
What does that mean?
SERVER ADMIN 2
Basically sir, gi-flood sa attacker ang
website ug fake traffic. Ang server nato

Activate rate limiting. Reduce repeated

sources.
SERVER ADMIN 2
I'll contact our security provider. They
can redirect the traffic through a
scrubbing service before it reaches our
server.
